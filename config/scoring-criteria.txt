CLEARWATER COMMERCIAL CLEANING - LEAD SCORING CRITERIA
Last updated: 22 September 2026

This is the text the AI reads before scoring every lead. It is written in plain
English on purpose: change it and the next lead is scored by the new rules, with
nobody touching the automation.

This file is the template. The live copy is the Google Doc
"Clearwater - Lead Scoring Criteria", which the workflow reads on every run.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WE DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We clean offices and commercial premises in the Portland, Oregon metro area.
Our work is recurring contracts - nightly, three times a week, or weekly. One-off
cleans are accepted only when they may lead to a contract.

We do not clean private homes or apartments.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT MAKES A LEAD HOT (score 8-10)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• A business, not a private individual.
• Premises over 5,000 sq ft, or more than 20 staff.
• Wants recurring cleaning, not a one-off.
• In the Portland metro area (including Beaverton, Hillsboro, Gresham, Lake Oswego,
  Tigard, Vancouver WA).
• Wants to start within 30 days.
• Names a budget, or asks for a walkthrough or a quote.

A lead that ticks most of these is hot even if one detail is missing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT MAKES A LEAD WARM (score 5-7)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• A business, but smaller premises (1,000-5,000 sq ft).
• Interested in recurring cleaning but has no firm start date.
• "Getting quotes", comparing suppliers, or planning next quarter's budget.
• In the service area but vague about what they need.
• A large one-off job (a post-construction or move-out clean of commercial premises)
  that could become a contract.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT MAKES A LEAD COLD (score 1-4)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Private homes, apartments or domestic cleaning of any kind.
• Outside the Portland metro area with no plans to open here.
• Job seekers asking about work, and anyone selling us something.
• A one-off clean of a small space with no interest in anything further.
• Too vague to act on: no company, no location, no indication of what is needed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIER THRESHOLDS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

score 8, 9, 10  = hot
score 5, 6, 7   = warm
score 1, 2, 3, 4 = cold

If the score and the tier ever disagree, the score wins.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO WRITE THE REASON
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every score comes with one sentence explaining it. Quote real details from the
lead, so whoever picks it up can judge it without opening the CRM.

Never invent facts about a lead. If the lead says nothing about size, do not guess
a size. Score what is actually there, and say what is missing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTE ON THE OUTPUT FORMAT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The JSON shape the AI must return is set in the workflow's prompt, not here.
That is deliberate: a client editing their criteria cannot accidentally break the
format and stop the pipeline.
